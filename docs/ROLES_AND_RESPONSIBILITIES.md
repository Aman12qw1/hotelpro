# HotelPro (Resort Edition) - Roles & Responsibilities (RBAC Matrix)

HotelPro Resort Edition enforces Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across three specialized hospitality tiers:

---

## 1. Role Definitions

### 👤 1. Honored Guest (VIP Traveler)
* **Goal:** Discover and reserve overwater villas, customize Ayurvedic spa rituals, summon private butler services, and track eco-contributions.
* **Permissions:**
  * Explore private villa catalogs, launch 360° visualizers.
  * Customize stay with spa packages and floating champagne breakfasts.
  * Summon private island butler (buggy, rose petal bath, pillow menu).
  * Reserve spots in complimentary daily wellness classes.
  * Access verified gold-sealed reservation vouchers and UNESCO Eco-Passports.

### 🛎️ 2. Island Butler / Operations Staff
* **Goal:** Maintain luxury sanctuary readiness and attend to in-villa guest requests in real time.
* **Permissions:**
  * Access live Villa Readiness & Housekeeping status table.
  * Toggle villa inspection status from `Prepared & Available` to `VIP Welcome Ready`.
  * Monitor incoming guest service requests and mark them as `Completed`.
  * *Restrictions:* Cannot modify villa pricing or view overall resort financial turnover.

### 🛡️ 3. General Manager (Executive Admin)
* **Goal:** Direct resort financial growth, environmental impact compliance, and seasonal inventory yields.
* **Permissions:**
  * View executive analytics: Occupancy Ratio (92.8%), ADR (₹46,800), and monthly gross.
  * Modify base nightly tariffs dynamically and toggle peak festive surge rates.
  * Oversee Oasis Pearls loyalty wallet balances and VIP tier upgrades.
