# HotelPro Luxury Eco-Resort - System UI Showcase & Feature Verification

This visual showcase outlines the primary user interfaces, guest booking workflows, and administrative dashboards across the **HotelPro Luxury Eco-Resort & Spa Sanctuary** platform:

---

### 📸 Core Interfaces for Demonstration & Verification:

1. **Homepage & Hero Sanctuary Banner**
   * *View:* The emerald green sanctuary hero header, "100% Solar Powered" top badge, and the 5-field floating search bar.
2. **Villa Catalog & Category Filter Tabs**
   * *View:* Filter tabs (`All Sanctuaries`, `Overwater Ocean Villas`, `Beachfront Pool Villas`, `Ayurvedic Wellness Retreats`) with villa cards displaying pricing in INR.
3. **360° Sanctuary Perspective Modal**
   * *Trigger:* Click "View 360 Sanctuary" on any villa.
   * *View:* Multi-angle switcher (Private Sun Deck, Infinity Plunge Pool, Open-Air Stone Tub) and full-bleed photography.
4. **Curated Ayurvedic Spa & Rituals Builder**
   * *View:* The Bodhi Tree Lotus Spa section with selected ritual checkboxes and real-time total calculator.
5. **Daily Retreat Master Schedule**
   * *View:* Daily timetable with "Reserve Spot" buttons showing 1-click confirmation badges.
6. **24/7 Island Butler Desk Simulator**
   * *View:* Assigned Butler profile card, 4 service request action buttons, and active dispatch notification.
7. **Certified UNESCO Eco-Passport & Carbon Tracker**
   * *View:* Gold seal certificate showing kg of CO2 offset, trees planted, and 100% plastic-free certification.
8. **Gold-Sealed Sanctuary Reservation Voucher**
   * *Trigger:* Complete reservation modal.
   * *View:* Double gold-bordered voucher with Reservation ID, breakdown, and "Save Sanctuary Pass" button.
9. **Butler Staff Operations Desk**
   * *Trigger:* Click "Butler / Staff" in the top role selector.
   * *View:* Villa readiness matrix and live incoming guest butler request queue.
10. **General Manager Executive Analytics**
    * *Trigger:* Click "GM Admin" in the top role selector.
    * *View:* 4 executive metric cards and the Dynamic Seasonal Pricing modifier table.
