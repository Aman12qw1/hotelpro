# HotelPro (Resort Edition) - Complete Features Specification

HotelPro (Resort & Spa Sanctuary Edition) delivers an ultra-luxury hospitality management platform specifically tailored for tropical island overwater villas and holistic wellness resorts.

---

## 1. Guest Experience (Sanctuary Explorer)
* **360° Perspective Sanctuary Viewer:**
  * Toggle between Private Sun Deck, Overwater Infinity Plunge Pool, Open-Air Stone Bath, and Glass-Floor Bedroom.
* **Curated Ayurvedic Spa & Experience Package Builder:**
  * Dynamic price calculation for Balinese herbal compresses (+₹5,200), floating champagne ocean breakfast (+₹3,500), private sunset catamaran cruise (+₹8,500), and candlelight beachfront pergola dinner (+₹6,000).
* **Certified UNESCO Eco-Passport & Carbon Offset Tracker:**
  * Real-time calculation of guest stay footprint: 22kg CO2 offset per night, native Baa Atoll mangroves planted, and coral micro-fragmentation registry certificate.
* **Daily Retreat Master Calendar & 1-Click RSVP:**
  * Sunrise Vinyasa yoga on the ocean deck, cold-pressed organic matcha elixir workshop, coral reef nursery snorkel safari, and Tibetan sound bath meditation.
* **Web Audio Ocean Waves Soundscape:**
  * Built-in Web Audio synthesis producing gentle, soothing ocean wave chimes with active frequency visualizer.
